  174. 174 · FAQ · What should I do first?Build your metal, crystal and gas extractors and solar power, then follow the quests on the Quests tab. I'll always point to the next step on your path, so you're never stuck.
  175. 175 · FAQ · What is the Academy?The Academy is your guided start. Each step teaches one system, from building your first extractor to winning your first battles, and pays a reward. Finish it and the whole galaxy opens up.
  176. 176 · FAQ · Does my empire grow while I'm away?Yes. Your extractors keep producing, and your building, research and shipyard queues keep running while you're offline, up to your storage limits.
  177. 177 · FAQ · Am I safe as a new commander?For your first three days, or until you reach 300 score, other commanders can't attack your colonies. Use that time to learn against the Hive and build your defenses.
  178. 178 · FAQ · What are the resources for?Metal and crystal build almost everything. Gas fuels your flights and advanced technology. Energy from solar and fusion plants keeps your extractors running at full speed.
  179. 179 · FAQ · Why is my production low?Check your energy. When your plants make less energy than your extractors need, every extractor slows down. Build more solar or a fusion reactor, and raise your storage so nothing overflows.
  180. 180 · FAQ · What are Void Shards?Void Shards are the rare currency. You earn them from bosses, pyramid vaults, shard mines and events, and spend them in the Void Shop on boosters, accelerators, items and VIP.
  181. 181 · FAQ · How do I get more planets?Research Astrophysics. Every two levels lets you hold one more world, up to ten. Then send a Colony Ship to settle or capture one. A new planet is shielded for seven days.
  182. 182 · FAQ · How do battles work?A battle lasts up to eight rounds. Every ship fires its own weapons; shields soak damage first, then armor and hull take the rest. You can watch every fight as a replay from Mail.
  183. 183 · FAQ · Which ships should I build?Every ship has a counter. Cruisers beat light fighters, dreadnoughts beat battleships, bombers beat defenses, destroyers beat dreadnoughts, and reapers beat capital ships. Mix your fleet.
  184. 184 · FAQ · What happens to ships I lose?Repair docks recover thirty percent of the ships you lose in any battle, and seventy percent of destroyed defenses are rebuilt. A bad fight hurts, but it won't end your empire.
  185. 185 · FAQ · Why can't I send another fleet?All your fleet slots are busy. Research Fleet Command, settle more colonies, or wait for a fleet to come home. Every colony adds a slot.
  186. 186 · FAQ · Why do flights cost gas?Every flight burns gas, more for long trips and heavy ships. Flying slower saves a lot of fuel, and the Coalition Depot cuts the fuel for every flight.
  187. 187 · FAQ · How do I spy on a planet?Build espionage probes, then tap Espionage on the planet's card. More probes reveal more: the fleet, then defenses, buildings and research. Better Espionage Technology helps too.
  188. 188 · FAQ · What is a joint strike?A joint strike lets allies attack together. Start one on a strong target, your allies join before the timer runs out, and every fleet fights as one.
  189. 189 · FAQ · What is the Finder?The Finder is your map search. Tap a box, like pirates, Hive worlds, mines or map events, and I'll highlight the nearest targets for you.
  190. 190 · FAQ · What are the layers?The galaxy has six layers around the black hole. Each layer inward has stronger enemies and richer rewards. New commanders start in the outer layers.
  191. 191 · FAQ · What are pirate hideouts?Pirate hideouts are full of stolen cargo. Beat the crew and your holds fill with their loot. They regroup after a raid, so come back later.
  192. 192 · FAQ · What are pyramid stations?Derelict pyramid stations hold vaults of resources, shards and items. You can loot each one once a day, and from level three something still guards the halls.
  193. 193 · FAQ · How do Void Shard mines work?Shard mines are rare fields that pay Void Shards. A dig is slow, each mine rests for a day after your dig, and you can mine up to five shards a day.
  194. 194 · FAQ · What are space monsters?Space monsters roam the map. Slay them for resources, shards and time accelerators that speed up your queues.
  195. 195 · FAQ · What are Assimilation Cubes?Assimilation Cubes are fortresses. One fleet rarely breaks them, so call a joint strike. They drop runes and the rarest forge materials.
  196. 196 · FAQ · What is cinematic view?Tap Cinematic view on a target's card and the camera flies around it. Drag to look around, and switch the angles at the bottom. World bosses get a full scan.
  197. 197 · FAQ · What is the Battle for the Super Cube?Every Friday at eighteen hundred UTC, for forty-eight hours, alliances fight to seize and hold the Super Cube. The longer your alliance holds it, the more glory and rewards you earn.
  198. 198 · FAQ · What are world bosses?The Pyramid Overlord, the Orbital Devourer and the Void Warden return every Monday at eighteen hundred UTC. Every hit pays a bounty, and when one falls, everyone who fought it shares the spoils.
  199. 199 · FAQ · What do the Energy Conduits do?Four conduits feed the Pyramid Overlord's shield. While all four stand, it takes only a quarter of the damage. Destroy them first.
  200. 200 · FAQ · What is Galaxy War?Galaxy War is a battle tournament every two weeks, from Monday to Wednesday UTC. Every battle point you earn counts toward milestones and rank rewards.
  201. 201 · FAQ · Where are my daily rewards?Open Events. Claim your daily login reward, the Online Bonus for time played, and the Level Bonus as you grow. The Daily Challenge adds more.
  202. 202 · FAQ · Why join an alliance?Allies defend your worlds, join your strikes and fight beside you for the Super Cube. Alliance bonuses make every member stronger.
  203. 203 · FAQ · How do I join an alliance?Open the Alliance tab, browse the list and ask to join one, or found an alliance of your own and invite your friends.
  204. 204 · FAQ · What is VIP?VIP raises your production and gives you free speed-ups. Earn VIP points from daily logins, events and shard purchases, and switch VIP on in the Void Shop.
  205. 205 · FAQ · What are runes?Runes go into elemental rings. Fire boosts attack, water shields, earth armor, air speed, and spirit your resource output. They drop from Cube bosses and world bosses.
  206. 206 · FAQ · What is a Teleport Ticket?A Teleport Ticket moves one of your planets to an empty orbit. Tap an empty orbit on the map to use it.
  207. 207 · FAQ · What does my commander class do?The Warlord runs two extra fleets and flies warships faster on less fuel. The Industrialist carries more cargo and flies cargo ships faster. The Explorer flies frigates faster.
  208. 208 · FAQ · What is my flagship?Your flagship leads your fleets into battle. Upgrade it in Flagship Command with forge materials from monsters, Cubes and vaults.
  209. 209 · FAQ · Is my progress saved?Yes. When you play signed in, your empire saves to the cloud as you go, so you can carry on from another device.
  210. 210 · FAQ · The game feels slow. What can I do?Open Settings and lower the graphics: render scale and effects. Phones run smoother on lighter settings, and the game remembers your choice.
  211. 211 · FAQ · How do I turn your voice off?Open Settings and switch Advisor voice off. You can turn the music and sound effects on or off there too.
  212. 212 · FAQ · How do I report a problem?Tap Feedback, describe what happened, and send it. Crashes and slowdowns are reported to the team automatically as well.
