  143. 143 · FAQ · What should I do first?Build your metal, crystal and gas extractors and solar power, then follow the quests on the Quests tab. I'll always point to the next step on your path, so you're never stuck.
  144. 144 · FAQ · What is the Academy?The Academy is your guided start. Each step teaches one system, from building your first extractor to winning your first battles, and pays a reward. Finish it and the whole galaxy opens up.
  145. 145 · FAQ · Does my empire grow while I'm away?Yes. Your extractors keep producing, and your building, research and shipyard queues keep running while you're offline, up to your storage limits.
  146. 146 · FAQ · Am I safe as a new commander?For your first three days, or until you reach 300 score, other commanders can't attack your colonies. Use that time to learn against the Hive and build your defenses.
  147. 147 · FAQ · What are the resources for?Metal and crystal build almost everything. Gas fuels your flights and advanced technology. Energy from solar and fusion plants keeps your extractors running at full speed.
  148. 148 · FAQ · Why is my production low?Check your energy. When your plants make less energy than your extractors need, every extractor slows down. Build more solar or a fusion reactor, and raise your storage so nothing overflows.
  149. 149 · FAQ · What are Void Shards?Void Shards are the rare currency. You earn them from bosses, pyramid vaults, shard mines and events, and spend them in the Void Shop on boosters, accelerators, items and VIP.
  150. 150 · FAQ · How do I get more planets?Research Astrophysics. Every two levels lets you hold one more world, up to ten. Then send a Colony Ship to settle or capture one. A new planet is shielded for seven days.
  151. 151 · FAQ · How do battles work?A battle lasts up to eight rounds. Every ship fires its own weapons; shields soak damage first, then armor and hull take the rest. You can watch every fight as a replay from Mail.
  152. 152 · FAQ · Which ships should I build?Every ship has a counter. Cruisers beat light fighters, dreadnoughts beat battleships, bombers beat defenses, destroyers beat dreadnoughts, and reapers beat capital ships. Mix your fleet.
  153. 153 · FAQ · What happens to ships I lose?Repair docks recover thirty percent of the ships you lose in any battle, and seventy percent of destroyed defenses are rebuilt. A bad fight hurts, but it won't end your empire.
  154. 154 · FAQ · Why can't I send another fleet?All your fleet slots are busy. Research Fleet Command, settle more colonies, or wait for a fleet to come home. Every colony adds a slot.
  155. 155 · FAQ · Why do flights cost gas?Every flight burns gas, more for long trips and heavy ships. Flying slower saves a lot of fuel, and the Coalition Depot cuts the fuel for every flight.
  156. 156 · FAQ · How do I spy on a planet?Build espionage probes, then tap Espionage on the planet's card. More probes reveal more: the fleet, then defenses, buildings and research. Better Espionage Technology helps too.
  157. 157 · FAQ · What is a joint strike?A joint strike lets allies attack together. Start one on a strong target, your allies join before the timer runs out, and every fleet fights as one.
  158. 158 · FAQ · What is the Finder?The Finder is your map search. Tap a box, like pirates, Hive worlds, mines or map events, and I'll highlight the nearest targets for you.
